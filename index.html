About me

I'm a telecommunications engineer and business graduate specialising in IoT and energy efficiency, with experience in Spain, Poland, the United States and Japan.

What interests me most is the point where a new technology becomes a business, and where a business crosses a border. I've seen it from several sides. I spent three years developing IoT and energy-efficiency projects in Madrid, supported international ICT teams at Acciona and wrote start-up plans that won student competitions. Now I'm in Tokyo, doing internationalization research for European and Japanese companies.

I know I'm early in my career, and I'm here to keep learning. My goal is to grow into business leadership, either by building a company of my own or by leading teams inside a technology company that creates new solutions in IoT, energy or smart cities. An MBA is likely part of that path.

Wherever it takes me, I'll keep travelling. It's how I learn fastest.
